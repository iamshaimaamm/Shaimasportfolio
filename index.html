— About

Shaimaa Mohsen UI/UX & Product Designer

I'm Shaimaa Mohsen a UI/UX & Product Designer based in Cairo, Egypt.

I'm the kind of designer who asks "why" before touching Figma. I care about the person on the other side of the screen what they're trying to do, where they get stuck, and what's actually stopping them from getting there.

With 4+ years working across UX, web design, and real client projects, I've learned that good design has to work before it can look good. My background in web development means I understand how designs actually get built which makes me easier to work with and harder to disappoint.

I don't just design screens. I design experiences that make sense to the people using them, not just the people building them.
